THE PROBLEM

Compressor faults are found when the machine stops

One person, many machines

In a small or mid-sized factory, one maintenance lead covers the whole floor.

Data logged, nobody watching

Pressure, temperature and motor current are recorded, but no one reads them until something breaks.

Know-how sits in binders

The manual and past repair notes exist, but nobody searches them under pressure.

Intended user: the maintenance lead, who decides whether a machine keeps running.

Air leaks are the most common compressor fault. They raise energy use and overheat the machine, and they are usually found only when pressure drops far enough to stop the line.
