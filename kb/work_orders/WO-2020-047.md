# WO-2020-047 — Check valve seat worn, reservoir pressure drop between cycles (SYNTHETIC)
- **Asset:** APU-300 #2 · **Opened:** 2020-03-19 · **Closed:** 2020-03-20 · **Priority:** High
- **Trigger:** reservoir pressure falling quickly when compressor unloaded; oil temperature trending up.
- **Findings:** TP3 and Reservoirs averaging 0.4 bar lower; motor current average up 30 %; air back-flow
  through check valve heard at unload.
- **Action:** replaced check valve (CV-30). Oil temperature returned to 62 °C average.
- **Downtime:** 3 h. **Lesson:** a worn check valve looks like a leak on the sensors; listen at unload.
