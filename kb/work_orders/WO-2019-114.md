# WO-2019-114 — Air leak at reservoir drain valve (SYNTHETIC)
- **Asset:** APU-300 #2 · **Opened:** 2019-11-04 · **Closed:** 2019-11-04 · **Priority:** High
- **Trigger:** operator noticed compressor running almost continuously.
- **Findings:** duty cycle ~68 % (normal 30 %), 14 starts/hour. Pressure decay test with consumers
  isolated: 10.0 → 7.9 bar in 9 min. Soap test found reservoir auto-drain valve stuck open.
- **Action:** replaced auto-drain valve (part DV-12). Decay test after repair: 10.0 → 9.7 bar in 10 min.
- **Downtime:** 1.5 h. **Root cause:** debris in drain valve seat.
